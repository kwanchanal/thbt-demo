Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about THBT and your wallet.

What is THBT?

THBT (Thai Baht TrueMoney) is a digital token issued by TrueMoney Co., Ltd. on the public Base blockchain, with its value pegged 1:1 to the Thai baht. Every THBT token issued is backed 100% by fiat currency securely held in bank accounts, with regular audits conducted to ensure transparency and stability.

THBT is a pilot project under the Programmable Payment testing in the Enhanced Regulatory Sandbox, operating from August 4, 2025, to December 31, 2026. The project is limited to 20,000 users throughout its duration, with a maximum issuing limit of 10,000 tokens per user per month.

Who is the issuer of THBT?

TrueMoney Co., Ltd. is the issuer of Thai Baht TrueMoney (THBT) under the Programmable Payment testing project in the Enhanced Regulatory Sandbox.

Which blockchain is THBT developed on?

THBT is developed on the Base chain, and has been fully audited by external assessors.

What is the value of THBT?

1 THBT is equivalent to 1 Thai baht.

Once users successfully register for the THBT service, they can issue or withdraw THBT via the TrueMoney app. During this pilot period, the company will not charge any fees. When withdrawing THBT, users will receive the full amount back in Thai baht based on the quantity of THBT they wish to withdraw.

Where can THBT be used, and who is eligible to use it?

Users can issue or withdraw THBT tokens via the TrueMoney application. Registration for the service is available from August 4, 2025, to December 31, 2026. Please note that the total number of users throughout the project is limited to 20,000.
